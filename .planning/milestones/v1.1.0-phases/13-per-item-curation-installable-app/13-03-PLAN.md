<objective>
Give the stream desktop-standard multi-select, a floating bulk-action bar, and the excluded-bucket toggle — the whole user-facing half of KERN-09/KERN-10.

Purpose: the kernel already partitions a webspace into two buckets and can write marks in bulk. This plan is where per-item curation stops being an API and becomes something a user can actually do at speed: select a run of rows, exclude them in one click, undo in one more, and go look at what they removed.
Output: multi-select on `StreamRow`, `SelectionActionBar`, the `Excluded (N)` toggle in the webspace header, the include mirror on the detail pane, and Playwright specs covering both round trips.
</objective>

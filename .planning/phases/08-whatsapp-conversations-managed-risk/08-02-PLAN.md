<objective>
Make the WhatsApp plugin degrade honestly: name de-link, ban, and session expiry as three distinct health states, guarantee that none of them ever empties the stream, widen matching to 1:1 chats on the user's own saved contact names, and lock the read-only boundary down with AST-scan tests.

Purpose: SRC-03's criterion 4 — the whole "managed risk" premise — hinges on the plugin telling the kernel "I cannot answer" rather than "nothing matched". This plan is where that guarantee is built and proved, and where D-05/D-06/D-07 widen the match surface.

Output: a plugin that goes red with a specific, honest reason and keeps every previously captured message browsable.
</objective>

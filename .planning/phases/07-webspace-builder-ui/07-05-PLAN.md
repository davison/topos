<objective>
**As a** person maintaining my topos setup, **I want to** one place to rename, delete or repair a source instance, delete a webspace I no longer want, and pull in a hand-edit — **so that** the parts of configuration that do not belong on a chip still have a home, and I never need to restart the service to apply a change I made in a text editor.

Purpose: 07-04 put composition on the chip row. This plan adds the deliberately minimal escape hatch D-13 specifies, gives the phase its two genuinely destructive actions the confirmation weight they warrant, pins the shared save/reload state pattern across every surface built in this phase, and publishes the contracts a hand-editing operator and a third-party plugin author read.

Output: the manage-sources modal, both AlertDialog confirmations, the reload affordance, a cross-component guard on the save-state pattern, and the updated `docs/api.md`, `config.example.toml` and `README.md`.
</objective>

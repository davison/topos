<objective>
Close 13-UAT.md's single gap G-13-1: clicking Undo after switching webspaces strands four permanent loading skeletons in the webspace the user navigated to.

The reversal itself is correct — 13-07 fixed that, and the kernel-side assertions prove it. What 13-07 also did was start calling `load(gen)` with a deliberately-stale generation, on the documented-but-false assumption that a stale-generation `load()` is a no-op. It is not: `load` writes `loadState = 'loading'` BEFORE its staleness guard, and the post-await guard then skips every exit that could set `loadState` back to `'ready'` or `'error'`. One-way transition into the loading state; four skeleton rows until the page is reloaded or Refresh all is clicked.

Purpose: make the assumption true at the source instead of relying on it. A single entry guard in `load` closes the failure for every caller, present and future — and the browser spec that let this ship gets the view-layer half it deliberately skipped.

Output: a `load` that genuinely no-ops when its generation is stale; a regression spec that drives the exact UAT reproduction (empty second webspace) and asserts on rendered stream state, not only kernel state; and three corrected records of the false claim.
</objective>

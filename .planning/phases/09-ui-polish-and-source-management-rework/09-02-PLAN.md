<objective>
Expand the tracer's proven icon path from one plugin to all seven: each
remaining in-repo plugin embeds and declares its own identity icon, with
recorded licensing provenance, and the WhatsApp pairing surface is branded as
topos rather than borrowing any WhatsApp/Meta mark.

This plan is deliberately four tasks rather than the usual three: the >5-files
per-task ceiling binds harder here than the tasks-per-plan guideline, because
the work is six repetitions of one two-file pattern (asset + Describe wiring)
that cannot be compressed without producing an unreviewable task. The final
task is a blocking human checkpoint — icon legibility at 14px and third-party
trademark policy are the two things in this phase a machine genuinely cannot
decide.

Purpose: satisfy ROADMAP success criterion 3 (every plugin visually
identifiable; WhatsApp de-branded) on the contract 09-01 already proved.
Output: six embedded icon assets, a repo-wide provenance audit, and a
topos-branded pairing panel with browser-level Playwright coverage.
</objective>
